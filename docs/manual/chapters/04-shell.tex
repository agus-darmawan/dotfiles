\chapter{The shell}\label{ch:shell}

zsh is configured in \file{zsh/.config/zsh/}; the files load in name order (\file{00-env.zsh},
\file{10-options.zsh}, \ldots, \file{80-ros.zsh}). Machine-specific settings go into
\file{~/.zshrc.local}, which is loaded last and not in git.

\section{Prompt and title}

The prompt is one line of plain ASCII, without icons:

\begin{plain}
dotfiles main* > _
patrol_controller main humble #42 12s > _
\end{plain}

\begin{keys}{3.4cm}
\head{Part}{Meaning}
\texttt{dotfiles} & The current folder (last path component only)\\
\texttt{main*} & Git branch, then \texttt{*} modified, \texttt{+} staged, \texttt{?} untracked,
\texttt{\^{}}/\texttt{v} ahead/behind, \texttt{!} conflict\\
\texttt{humble \#42} & ROS distro and \texttt{ROS\_DOMAIN\_ID}, or \texttt{noetic @go2} for a ROS~1
master. Only where ROS is sourced (containers, robots)\\
\texttt{12s} & How long the last command took, when it took more than 2 seconds\\
\texttt{>} & Green, or red after a failed command\\
\end{keys}

The host is not in the prompt. Instead, zsh sets the window and tab title to
\texttt{host:path}, for example \texttt{macbook:\textasciitilde/apps/molly} or
\texttt{humble:\textasciitilde/ros2\_ws}. Ghostty, iTerm2, VS Code and tmux show it, and a tmux
window connected over SSH is named after the remote host.

\section{Line editing}

\begin{keys}{4cm}
\head{Keys}{Action}
\key{Tab} & Completion menu with fuzzy search and previews (folders, files, containers, SSH hosts); \key{<} / \key{>} switch groups\\
\key{Right} or \key{C-Space} & Accept the grey suggestion from history\\
\key{Up} / \key{Down}, \key{C-p} / \key{C-n} & Search history for what is already typed\\
\key{C-r} & Fuzzy search the whole history\\
\key{C-t} & Fuzzy-find a file and paste its path\\
\key{M-c} & Fuzzy \texttt{cd} into a subfolder\\
\key{C-x C-e} & Edit the current command in Neovim\\
\key{M-b} / \key{M-f} & Jump a word back / forward\\
\end{keys}

\section{Navigation and files}

\begin{keys}{3.8cm}
\head{Command}{Action}
\cmd{cd <part>} & zoxide: jump to the best match among visited folders (\cmd{cd molly})\\
\cmd{cdi} & zoxide, interactive\\
\cmd{..} & \cmd{cd ..} (typing a folder name alone changes into it)\\
\cmd{ls}, \cmd{ll}, \cmd{la} & eza with icons; long; long with hidden files and git status\\
\cmd{lt}, \cmd{lT} & Tree, 2 or 4 levels, git-ignored files hidden\\
\cmd{cat <file>} & bat, with syntax colours, no pager\\
\cmd{take <dir>} & \cmd{mkdir -p} and \cmd{cd}\\
\cmd{ex <archive>} & Extract tar, zip, gz, xz, zst or 7z\\
\cmd{v}, \cmd{vi}, \cmd{vim} & Neovim\\
\cmd{vc} / \cmd{dot} & Open the dotfiles in Neovim / \cmd{cd ~/dotfiles}\\
\end{keys}

\section{Git}

\begin{keys}{4.6cm}
\head{Alias}{Command}
\cmd{g} / \cmd{lg} & git / lazygit\\
\cmd{gs}, \cmd{gd}, \cmd{gds} & Status, diff, staged diff\\
\cmd{ga}, \cmd{gaa}, \cmd{gc}, \cmd{gcm "msg"}, \cmd{gca} & Add, add all, commit, commit with message, amend without editing\\
\cmd{gco}, \cmd{gsw}, \cmd{gcb <name>} & Checkout, switch, new branch\\
\cmd{gl} / \cmd{gf} & Pull / fetch all and prune\\
\cmd{glog} & Graph of the last 20 commits\\
\cmd{gst} / \cmd{gstp} & Stash / stash pop\\
\end{keys}

Diffs go through delta when it is installed and plain \texttt{less} otherwise (containers and
robots without delta).

\section{System helpers}

\begin{keys}{3.8cm}
\head{Command}{Action}
\cmd{myip} & Local IP addresses (handy on robot networks)\\
\cmd{ports} & Listening TCP ports and their processes\\
\cmd{serve} & Static HTTP server in the current folder\\
\cmd{rsy <src> <dst>} & \cmd{rsync -avhP}\\
\cmd{top} & btop\\
\cmd{path} & One \texttt{\$PATH} entry per line\\
\cmd{zr} & Reload zsh\\
\cmd{brewup} & Update all Homebrew packages\\
\cmd{flushdns} & Flush the macOS DNS cache\\
\cmd{plug-update} & Update the zsh plugins\\
\end{keys}

\begin{note}[Paste one command at a time]
When several lines are pasted at once and one of them is \cmd{exec zsh}, the shell is replaced and
the lines after it are lost. Run commands that restart the shell on their own.
\end{note}
